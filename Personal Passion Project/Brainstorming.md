
## Initial Idea splurging

Slow, casual experience
Heavily influenced by the real world of plants, animals, biology and ecosystems
Analogue time setting, pre-2k; polaroid camera, cassette recorder, paper journal etc
*Procedural behaviour?*
Studying the environment, how the plants grow, how the animals interact with the plants and each other, record findings with a journal and equipment
Idea to have different puzzles involving specific plants/mechanics/animals etc.

The main inspiration comes from wanting a slowed down experience, especially in today's climate of entertainment/videogames where everything is fast paced and the focus is on digital domination. I want an experience where you can sit down in an area and just experience a natural ecosystem and life cycle, where you are rewarded for patience and just recording your experience however you want. 
* An example would be setting up an audio recording explaining what you are seeing with how birds interact with a plant and act as a pollinator for the plant, when playing back the recording you can also hear the environment as well as the recorded voice.
I want the feeling of actually being in a working ecosystem that changes and evolves and adapts over time, especially to the presence of a human.
The procedural inspiration primarily comes from rainworld and of course real life.
Glorify nature
Love letter to nature
