# USP's

Combination of top-down hack and slash with werewolf and time-travel aesthetics
There is a gap in the market for top-down hack and slash games that aren't roguelikes.

Time travel as a theme is either used in a videogames narrative or in decision making rather than in an action game during combat. A few games that use time travel as an active mechanic are online multiplayer competitive games such as "Overwatch" and "League of Legends". 

One of the USP's is having the player able to manipulate time while in action, adding coherence between narrative and gameplay.


A cohesive theming on time-travel between narrative and gameplay.
Action packed top-down singleplayer gameplay without and roguelike or roguelite elements.
Unique combination of hack and slash with werewolf and vampire aesthetics
